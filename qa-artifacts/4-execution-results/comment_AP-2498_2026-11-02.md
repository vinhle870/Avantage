*Bugs:*
* AP-2523 — The roadside-protection modal shows "No road side protection products are available for this vehicle." instead of "In order to get a quote for road side protection, please specify the make and model of the vehicle." (Medium, Backlog). The notice is correctly placed below the form; only the text is wrong.
* AP-2524 — When products are listed, the roadside-protection modal folds the vehicle form (VIN, Make, Model, Year, Continue) behind an "Edit" link (Medium, Backlog). The title says "keep the existing vehicle form visible" and the criteria say "The existing form is always visible" and "the form does not disappear because make/model are complete". The same pattern is reported on the extended-warranty modal in AP-2521 and likely has a shared cause. *Please confirm on this ticket whether the folded layout is intended.*
* AP-2525 — The VIN field of the roadside-protection form is not required: an empty-VIN Continue returns prices and saves the Make and Model (Medium, Backlog). The ticket says "the VIN input is required" and "a required VIN".

* Q-009 (Judgement) — Should Make/Model prefill from the vehicle when nothing is stored? — They are not prefilled (they show "Choose").
* Q-010 (Judgement) — Does the submit also change the inventory vehicle? — No, only the roadside program.

* Q-012 (Judgement) — Which VIN does the product section and the contract use? — Not checkable on this build (AP-2508).
