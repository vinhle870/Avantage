The simplified "Create a quote" vehicle form works on dev. It shows only Quote name, VIN, Brand, Model and Year. It saves a quote with Brand, Model and Year alone, and refuses a save that is missing one of them. The inventory form keeps its full fields. A quote made from the form opens its warranty step without an error. The VIN decoder does not work as specified: clicking "Décoder" saves a quote at once instead of filling in the form. That is filed as AP-2507, and it also blocked two related test cases.
