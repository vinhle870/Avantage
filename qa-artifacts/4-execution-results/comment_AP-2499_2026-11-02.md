The add-GAP and add-creditors insurance windows (the *+* on the quote's "Gap Insurance" and "Creditors insurance" rows) were tested with 13 test cases. On a quote with no financing, both windows now show the financing form instead of the "Please add a financing…" alert; saving a financing there stores all eight values and moves on to the GAP step without leaving the page; and missing or invalid values are refused with field errors and nothing is stored. Three defects in the same windows make five cases fail; they are filed below.

*Bugs:*
* AP-2526 — Once a quote has a financing, the GAP/creditors add modal folds the financing form into one summary line behind an "Edit" link (Medium, Backlog). The criteria say "the financing form does not disappear when a financing already exists" and "each field shows the stored value"; the stored values are correct after Edit. The same folding is reported on the extended-warranty (AP-2521) and roadside-protection (AP-2524) modals, probably a shared component.
* AP-2527 — Saving the financing in the creditors-insurance modal of a quote that already has a financing draws the whole quote page inside the modal (Medium, Backlog). The new amount (30000) is saved, but the modal does not refresh to the insurance step.
* AP-2528 — "Get Quote" in the GAP/creditors add modal reloads the quote page and closes the modal, so the insurance result is never shown below the form (Medium, Backlog). The quote is saved and its premium shows on the Summary-of-products row. Related to AP-2410, which reports an error on the same Get Quote; on Dev the submit succeeds.

*Not tested yet:*
* *AP2499_005 is BLOCKED by Q-011.* The financing form refuses an empty residual with "This value should not be blank.", so a financing without a residual cannot be stored and the prefill of an empty residual cannot be checked. *Question Q-011 for you: is the residual meant to be optional?* The Validation criterion lists type, base amount, payment frequency, term, interest rate and financing company, but not the residual or the date of birth. Today the residual is required and the date of birth is not flagged.
* *AP2499_009 is BLOCKED by Q-007.* To make the creditors quote fail, a Life term of 999 was tried; Chubb's development API priced it as a 60-month term (18.58$/m) with no error. So the "on failure an error is shown without discarding the entered values" branch was never exercised. This is recorded under Q-007, not as a bug. Please tell us how a GAP or creditors quote can be made to fail.
* *AP2499_013 is BLOCKED by Q-002.* No dealer configured for Humania exists on Dev, so the Humania half of "Financing form is always present above the insurance form" was not tested.
